Less searching • More teaching

Find and Evaluate OER Without Losing Hours to the Search

The OER Promptbook gives community college faculty ready-to-use AI prompts for the time-consuming parts of OER: finding promising resources, comparing options, filling gaps, and fitting materials into a course you already teach.

  • ✓ Start with a shortlist
  • ✓ Compare faster
  • ✓ Keep your course structure
No prompt-engineering expertise required. Each task is designed around a real faculty time crunch, with a simple copy-and-paste option for newer AI users and a deeper option for power users.

OER Can Save Students Money. It Shouldn't Eat Up Your Prep Time.

Use AI for the first-pass work that can make OER adoption feel overwhelming, then use your faculty judgment to decide what is actually worth teaching.

Too many places to search?

Ask AI to search broadly and bring back a manageable shortlist instead of opening repository after repository yourself.

Too much to compare?

Put promising resources side by side for coverage, course fit, currency, accessibility, and licensing before you spend hours reading.

Don't want to rebuild your course?

Map OER to the weeks, units, outcomes, and assignments you already use, or fill only the gaps you actually need to fill.

How It Works

  1. Choose the part of OER work that is costing you time.
  2. Pick the simple prompt or the deeper power-user version.
  3. Add your course information and copy the prompt into the AI tool you use.
  4. Review the shortlist, open the sources, verify important details, and decide what is worth using.

Let AI reduce the searching. Keep the academic judgment yours.
