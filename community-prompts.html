How the Community Lab Works

1

Someone Shares a Prompt

A short submission form collects the prompt, what it helps with, and a few optional details such as discipline and the AI tool used.

2

It Appears Here Automatically

Community submissions are pulled from a public data feed. The maintainer does not have to copy prompts into the website one at a time.

3

The Community Adds Signals

People can say a prompt worked for them or report a problem. These are community signals, not quality guarantees or formal endorsements.

See What People Are Trying

The Promptbook Showcase is a more informal space for screenshots, brief examples, adaptations, and reflections on what happened when people tried a prompt.

The community showcase will appear here once its Padlet embed URL is added.

Before You Share

Please remove student names, identifiable student work, grades, accommodations, confidential college information, private email addresses, and other sensitive information from prompts, screenshots, or examples.

Only share material you created, have permission to share, or are otherwise permitted to reuse.
