About This Site

Privacy

The OER Promptbook is designed to be a simple public resource. We do not ask you to create an account or provide personal information.

What This Site Collects

The OER Promptbook does not collect, store, or sell personal information. This site does not use accounts, forms, mailing lists, advertising trackers, or its own analytics.

GitHub Pages

This site is hosted through GitHub Pages. Like other web-hosting services, GitHub may automatically collect limited technical information when you visit, such as IP address, browser or device information, and access logs. That information is collected and handled by GitHub, not by The OER Promptbook.

External Links and AI Tools

If you follow a link from this site to another website or AI service, that service has its own privacy policies and data practices. The OER Promptbook does not control how external services collect or use information.

Privacy reminder: Avoid entering confidential student, employee, or institutional information into a public AI tool. Review your college or district policies and the privacy settings of the AI service you use.
