About This Site

Privacy

The OER Promptbook is a public, independently maintained faculty resource. I do not require an account to browse the core site.

What the Core Site Collects

I do not run a first-party account system, mailing list, advertising tracker, or site analytics for The OER Promptbook, and I do not sell personal information. You can browse the core Promptbook without submitting personal information to me.

Community Prompt Lab

The Community Prompt Lab uses external Google services. If you choose to submit a prompt, say that a prompt worked for you, or report a problem, the information you enter is collected through Google Forms and is also subject to Google's privacy practices.

Community prompt submissions are retrieved through a public Google Apps Script data feed. Prompt content and optional attribution information intended for display, such as a contributor name or institution, may appear publicly on the Community page. Do not submit information that you do not want displayed publicly.

I may review form responses as the volunteer maintainer in order to operate the Community Prompt Lab, respond to reports, and maintain the site.

Padlet Showcase

The Community page includes an embedded Padlet showcase and a link to Padlet. When you load or use that embedded service, Padlet may receive technical or usage information according to its own privacy practices. The Promptbook does not control Padlet's data collection.

GitHub Pages

This site is hosted through GitHub Pages. Like other web-hosting services, GitHub may automatically collect limited technical information when you visit, such as IP address, browser or device information, and access logs. That information is handled by GitHub under its own policies rather than by a Promptbook analytics system.

External Links and AI Tools

If you follow a link from this site to another website, repository, library service, or AI tool, that service has its own privacy policies and data practices. I do not control how external services collect or use information.

Privacy reminder: Avoid entering confidential student, employee, or institutional information into public AI tools, community forms, or public showcases. Review your college or district policies and the privacy settings of the services you use.

Questions and Maintenance

I, Karen Crozer, created and maintain The OER Promptbook independently on a volunteer basis.
